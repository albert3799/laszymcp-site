Everything a BDR needs

Six tools that replace your tab jungle. All inside the conversation.

🔍

Account Intelligence

CRM data, company enrichment, TheirStack hiring signals, and Claude web research -- merged into one dashboard per account, refreshed automatically.

🎯

Prospect Focus

Tag and filter the prospects that matter. Search by company, title, or focus state. Update BDR notes and focus directly from conversation.

📧

6-Touch Campaigns

Fixed 6-email sequences per prospect with status tracking. Claude drafts each touch, respects your playbook, and stages them as Gmail drafts.

🔄

Gmail Sync

Batch-sync sent messages from Gmail back into campaign stages. Laszy marks steps as sent and tracks replies -- your dashboard stays current without manual entry.

📚

Content Library

Query case studies, one-pagers, and decks by persona, pain point, or industry. Attach the right asset to the right email automatically.

🤝

Customer Matching

Find the most similar existing customers for any prospect. Match on industry, size, revenue, and geography for relevant social proof in every outreach.
